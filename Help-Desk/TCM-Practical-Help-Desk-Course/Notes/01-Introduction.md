# Introduction

## What is IT?

**IT (Information Technology)** is a broad term that can encompass many different groups and roles, for example software development or engineering.

**The traditional meaning of IT term** - responsible for administering, deploying, supporting technology that the organization uses to support business functions, which can include a wide range of both hardware and software, excluding things like software development and engineering.

-> One of the main roles of the IT department is to manage and support the technology required for the organization to function.

MSP - Managed Service department - takes care of all of the IT needs for a business.

## IT Department Responsibilities

**1. Infrastructure Management* - oversee installation, maintenance and upgrades of technological assets, including computers, phones, networking gear or maybe even cloud infrastructure.

**2. Tech support* - responsible for troubleshooting, training and administration tasks, helping the employees to effectively use the technology within the company.

**3. Security* - may not always mean cyber security, but installing security cameras, door locks, card readers, or other physical security stuff.

**4. Data Management* - the IT department is also responsible for the ways data within the company is stored: databases, cloud infrustructure, and so on. They are also responsible for access control and auditing for data. 

**5. Identity and access management* - manage users access to data and software, creating and administering accounts, setting up passwords.

**6. Project Management* - planning and managing IT projects, including upgrades or transitions to new solutions and strategic planning in order to keep business ready for new demands.

**7. Compliance and Governance* - ensures the organization adheres to regulations, standards and considerations such as privacy and data protection.
